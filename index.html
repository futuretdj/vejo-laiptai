    Questions people ask first.

    Is parking included?

    No, underground spaces are sold separately for €19,000, with an EV charger for €900 more. There are 46 spaces for 42 flats, and storage rooms in the basement from €3,500.

    What does A++ mean for my bills?

    Air-to-water heat pumps, underfloor heating, heat-recovery ventilation and triple glazing. A 2-room flat is expected to cost around €35–45 a month to heat in January.

    Can I change the layout?

    Until the walls go up on your floor, yes: moving or removing non-structural walls is free once and €450 per later change. Kitchens and bathrooms stay where the risers are.

    What if construction is late?

    The contract fixes a hand-over date. For each month of delay you are paid 0.5% of what you have already paid, and after six months you can cancel and get everything back.

    Who is the developer?

    A concept: this site is a portfolio demonstration. The building, prices and people are invented.
